'''Julian Perez''' (born 20 May 1982 in [[Valencia]]) is a Spanish [[DJ]] and electronic music producer based in [[Ibiza]], Spain. He is the founder of two vinyl-only record labels, Fathers & Sons Productions (2012) and Girada Unlimited (2017), and has released music on [[Cocoon Recordings]], Raum...musik, Recycle Records, Rawax and Subwax Bcn, among others.<ref name="ra-debut-lp">{{cite web |title=Julian Perez releases debut LP, closes Fathers & Sons Productions label |url=https://ra.co/news/36928 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |date=20 October 2016 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> Perez was named one of the Ibiza season's breakthrough talents by ''[[Mixmag]]'' in 2014<ref name="ra-ibiza-weekly">{{cite web |title=RA Ibiza weekly: Julian Perez, The Zoo Project |url=https://ra.co/news/26382 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |date=12 September 2014 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> and holds a summer residency at Underground Ibiza. He has performed at venues including Rex Club in Paris, Fabric in London, and Robert Johnson in Offenbach am Main, and at festivals including the [[Amsterdam Dance Event]], [[BPM Festival]], and Sunwaves Festival. He released his debut studio album, ''Solemnity'', in 2016; his second album, ''Break-Even'', in 2021; and his third album, ''A Journey Through The Beatscape'', in 2024. In 2026 he published his debut book, ''INTERFASE: The Infinite Set''.

==Career==

===Early career===
Julian Perez developed his connection with electronic music in the early 1990s, influenced by the house, [[techno]], EBM and New Beat scenes emerging from the United States, the United Kingdom and northern Europe. In an interview with ''[[XLR8R]]'', he recalled listening as a child to cassette tapes recorded at Valencia clubs including Barraca, Chocolate, Spook Factory and ACTV — key venues of the circuit known as La Ruta del Bakalao — and described that scene as a formative influence.<ref name="xlr8r-girada">{{cite web |title=Premiere: Hear Girada Unlimited's Cure Music Debut |url=https://xlr8r.com/news/premiere-hear-girada-unlimiteds-cure-music-debut/ |work=[[XLR8R]] |date=6 November 2018 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

From approximately 2000 to 2005, Perez worked in advertising and graphic design in [[Alicante]], Spain, while also performing as a DJ at clubs and events.<ref name="ra-ibiza-weekly" /> In 2006 he relocated to [[Ibiza]], where he began performing at after-parties and smaller venues on the island, eventually focusing on music production full-time. His first releases appeared around 2010, with appearances on compilations from labels including Leftroom and Be As One Imprint.

===Fathers & Sons Productions (2012–present)===
In 2012, Perez founded Fathers & Sons Productions, a [[vinyl record|vinyl-only]] record label based in Ibiza, and during this period also lived in [[Berlin]] until 2013.<ref name="ra-debut-lp" /> The label, described by ''[[Resident Advisor]]'' as known for "subtle, dubby house" music, released twelve records over four years, featuring artists including S.A.M., Livio & Roby, and Brian Harden.<ref name="ra-debut-lp" /> According to ''DJ Mag ES'', within its first year of operation the label organised showcases in more than twelve countries.<ref name="djmages-050">{{cite magazine |title=Focus On: Julian Perez |magazine=DJ Mag ES |issue=50 |date=March 2015}}</ref> During this period, Perez also released material on Steve Lawler's [[VIVa Music]] imprint, appeared on the ''Cocoon Compilation N'' (2014) and ''Zehn'' (2015) compilations on [[Cocoon Recordings]],<ref name="ra-cocoon-n">{{cite web |title=Cocoon Compiles N |url=https://ra.co/news/25431 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> and was involved in the [[tINI and the Gang]] touring collective.<ref name="ra-tini">{{cite web |title=tINI and The Gang Announces Five Ibiza Dates for 2016 |url=https://ra.co/news/34774 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

According to ''[[Mixmag]]'', his sound combined elements from Detroit and Chicago with a European dub techno sensibility, "a recipe that's won him support from the likes of [[Ricardo Villalobos]], Loco Dice, Apollonia and The Martinez Brothers."<ref name="mixmag-ibiza-2014">{{cite web |title=After the island: The breakout DJs of Ibiza of 2014 |url=http://mixmag.net/read/after-the-island-the-breakout-djs-of-ibiza-of-2014-blog |work=[[Mixmag]] |date=17 October 2014 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

A key release was ''FAS003'' (2012), which included his remix of "The Night Train", originally by Kadoc. ''DJ Mag Latinoamérica'' noted that the remix "hit hard in every club in Ibiza" during the summer of 2012.<ref name="djmag-latam">{{cite magazine |title=Exclusive interview: Julián Pérez |magazine=DJ Mag Latinoamérica |date=25 March 2013}}</ref> According to an interview in ''Vicious Magazine'', the very same afternoon Perez produced the remix, Loco Dice played it live at Amnesia Ibiza.<ref name="vicious-n33">{{cite magazine |title=Julián Pérez — Idoneidad Emprendedora |magazine=Vicious Magazine |issue=33 |date=May 2013}}</ref> The remix was later selected for the ''Vagabundos 2013'' and ''Vagabundos 2013 Part 2'' compilations on Cadenza, the label co-founded by Luciano and [[Ricardo Villalobos]].<ref name="discogs-fas003">{{cite web |title=FAS003 |url=https://www.discogs.com/release/4184082 |work=[[Discogs]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

In 2016, Perez made his debut on the Berlin-based Raum...musik imprint with the ''Now, After'' EP, described by ''[[Resident Advisor]]'' as "versatile material suitable for late-set club play."<ref name="ra-raum">{{cite web |title=Julian Perez – Now, After |url=https://ra.co/reviews/18750 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |author=Matt Unicomb |date=18 March 2016 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

Fathers & Sons Productions initially closed in late 2016 with the release of Perez's debut album, ''Solemnity'' (FAS012), a double [[LP record|LP]] spanning eleven tracks across four vinyl sides.<ref name="xlr8r-solemnity">{{cite web |title=Stream Snippets of Julian Perez's Debut Album |url=https://xlr8r.com/news/stream-snippets-of-julian-perezs-debut-album/ |work=[[XLR8R]] |date=16 December 2016 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> ''XLR8R'' described the album as featuring "floating soundscapes, electro-tinged 909 beats, spaced-out club weapons, and deep, dub techno."<ref name="xlr8r-solemnity" />

The label was reactivated in 2023 with ''FAS012+1'', followed by ''FAS014'' in 2025, both released by Perez.<ref name="discogs-fas">{{cite web |title=Fathers & Sons Productions discography |url=https://www.discogs.com/label/384754-Fathers-Sons-Productions |work=[[Discogs]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

===Girada Unlimited (2017–present)===
In 2017, Perez founded Girada Unlimited, a second vinyl-only label. The label was highlighted by ''[[XLR8R]]'' for its release on Cure Music, whose A-side "Zapatac" had become a regular in [[Ricardo Villalobos]]'s sets and featured in his BBC Radio 1 Essential Mix.<ref name="xlr8r-girada" /> The label has hosted releases by artists including Franco Cinelli and D&S. Its tenth release was Perez's second studio album, ''Break-Even'' (2021).<ref name="trommel-review">{{cite web |title=Review: Julian Perez – Break-Even LP [GIRADA010] |url=https://trommelmusic.com/music/reviews/review-julian-perez-break-even-lp-girada010/ |work=Trommel Music |date=17 May 2021 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> In 2024 he released his third album, ''A Journey Through The Beatscape'' (GIRADATAPES01), on cassette.<ref name="discogs-girada">{{cite web |title=Girada Unlimited discography |url=https://www.discogs.com/label/1008845-Girada-Unlimited |work=[[Discogs]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

===International career and recognition===
Perez has performed at clubs including Rex Club in Paris, Fabric in London, Womb in Tokyo, and Robert Johnson in Offenbach am Main. In Ibiza he is a regular resident at Underground Ibiza and has performed at events including ENTER. at Space Ibiza and Cocoon Ibiza.<ref name="ra-ibiza-weekly" />

He has performed at festivals including the [[Amsterdam Dance Event]],<ref name="ade">{{cite web |title=Julian Perez (ES) — Amsterdam Dance Event |url=https://www.amsterdam-dance-event.nl/en/artists-speakers/julian-perez/4659/ |work=Amsterdam Dance Event |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> the Sunwaves Festival, the Epizode Festival in Vietnam,<ref name="xlr8r-epizode">{{cite web |title=Epizode Festival Launches in Vietnam |url=https://xlr8r.com/news/epizode-festival-launches-in-vietnam/ |work=[[XLR8R]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> the [[BPM Festival]],<ref name="ra-bpm">{{cite web |title=BPM Festival: tINI & The Gang |url=https://ra.co/events/547990 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> the Outline Festival in Moscow,<ref name="ra-outline">{{cite web |title=Moscow's Outline Festival Announces 2016 Lineup |url=https://ra.co/news/35024 |work=[[Resident Advisor]] |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> the Sophie Festival in Málaga, off-[[Sónar]] parties in Barcelona, the Kazantip Festival, and the Winter Music Conference.

He has been interviewed and profiled in publications including ''[[Resident Advisor]]'', ''DJ Mag ES'', ''Vicious Magazine'', ''Ibiza Voice Magazine'' and ''Pulse Radio'', among others. He was nominated for Best Artist at the Vicious Music Awards in 2012<ref name="vicious-2012">{{cite web |title=Vicious Music Awards — Nominations 2012 |url=https://www.20minutos.es/noticia/1639126/0/premios-nacionales-electronica/vicious-musica-awards/juan-magan/ |work=20 Minutos |date=2012 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> and 2013,<ref name="vicious-2013">{{cite web |title=Vicious Music Awards — Nominations 2013 |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140209212223/http://www.viciousmagazine.com:80/viciousmusicawards/nominaciones.php? |work=Vicious Magazine |date=2013 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref> and for Best Newcomer at the DJ Awards in 2016.<ref name="dj-awards">{{cite web |title=DJ Awards 2016 — 19th Edition |url=https://djawards.com/editions/2016-19th-edition/ |work=DJ Awards |date=2016 |access-date=2026-06-27}}</ref>

===INTERFASE: The Infinite Set (2026)===
In 2026, Perez published ''INTERFASE: The Infinite Set'', his first book, released in English and Spanish. The work explores the ideas and experiences that shaped his career as a DJ and offers a perspective on his creative process in electronic music.<ref name="interfase-en">{{cite book |title=INTERFASE: The Infinite Set |author=Julian Perez |year=2026 |isbn=8409813726 |url=https://www.amazon.com/dp/8409813726 |access-date=2026-09-02}}</ref><ref name="interfase-es">{{cite book |title=INTERFASE: La sesión infinita |author=Julian Perez |year=2026 |isbn=8409794535 |url=https://www.amazon.com/dp/8409794535 |access-date=2026-09-02}}</ref>
